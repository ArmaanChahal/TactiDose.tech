      Good to know

        Assistant

        At the dispenser, say “Hey Care, give me my pill” or “Hey Care, when is my next pill?” Other phrases: “I took my pill,” “how many pills are left,” and “repeat that.” Say “Hey Care” before each new request. Talk and typing remain available for free-form conversation.

        Conversation with the assistant

          Shortcut: Alt + M. Press Talk again when you are done. Esc stops.

          Optional: a few questions about mood, stress and sleep. You are also asked after a pill drops. Saved answers appear in History, where your doctor and family can see them.

          Schedule

          Pills drop by themselves at these times, even if you forget. Only your doctor or family can change the schedule.

              History

              Every pill request, including the ones that did not drop and why.

              Well-being check-ins

              Your answers about mood, stress and sleep after your pills, with your own notes. Your doctor and family can see the ones you saved. You can delete any of them.

              Reports

              A report shows your pills, your schedule and your conversations for a period. You can send it to your doctor.

              Share with your care team
